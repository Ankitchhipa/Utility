Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Utility and its related mobile features handle information when you use cleaning, scanning, file, and productivity tools inside the app.

Last updated May 3, 2026
Applies to Utility mobile app
Contact support@mobibuz.com

1. Information we may collect

We may collect information needed to operate app features, improve reliability, and understand product performance. Depending on the feature you use, this may include:

  • Device and app information such as device model, OS version, app version, language, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage information such as screens visited, feature interactions, and high-level engagement events.
  • Files, images, scans, or documents you choose to process inside the app.
  • Support information you send to us directly through feedback or email.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve the app experience, including to:

  • Enable core features such as document scanning, file organization, junk cleaning, and sharing.
  • Maintain app security, detect abuse, and diagnose crashes or technical problems.
  • Measure product quality and understand which features are most helpful to users.
  • Respond to support requests or feature-related questions.
We do not need access to your private content unless you choose to scan, open, organize, share, or otherwise process that content through an app feature.

3. Permissions and device access

Some features request permissions so the app can work as expected. For example:

Camera for scanning Storage or file access for documents Notifications for important updates Network access for cloud-connected experiences

You can manage many permissions in your device settings. If a permission is denied, related features may be limited or unavailable.

4. Ads, analytics, and third parties

The app may use trusted third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, ads, or purchases. Those providers may process limited technical identifiers or diagnostic data according to their own privacy terms.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services made available through the app or its related SDKs.

5. Document and media handling

Files and images you choose inside the app are used to deliver the feature you requested, such as scanning, editing, compressing, signing, exporting, or sharing documents.

Where content is processed locally on your device, it stays under your device’s storage environment unless you explicitly export, share, back up, or sync that content elsewhere.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or support legitimate business operations.

7. Your choices

  • You can uninstall the app at any time.
  • You can manage app permissions through your device settings.
  • You can choose not to use certain features that require file, camera, or network access.
  • You can contact us if you have a privacy question or request.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law without parental or guardian supervision. If you believe information from a child has been provided improperly, please contact us.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or operational improvements. When we make material updates, we may revise the date shown at the top of this page.
